Best towing and repair services near Iowa City: what to look for

When your car breaks down, you rarely have time to research. This guide gives you a checklist to use before you need it, so you can pick a towing and repair service in the Iowa City area with confidence. We are Prairie Line Towing & Auto Repair, so we have included how we measure up too.

A seven-point checklist

  1. Answers 24/7. Breakdowns do not wait for business hours. Make sure a real person picks up at night, not only a voicemail box.
  2. Gives a price up front. A good company quotes the tow before the truck rolls, including any mileage charges.
  3. Arrives fast. Ask how long it usually takes to reach you. Ours averages 22 minutes in Iowa City.
  4. Is licensed and insured. Your vehicle is valuable. The company should carry insurance that covers it while in tow.
  5. Has the right trucks. All-wheel-drive and electric vehicles often need a flatbed. Ask before you book.
  6. Offers certified repair. Look for ASE-certified technicians and a written warranty on repairs.
  7. Has local reviews and roots. A company that has served your community for years has a reputation to protect.

Why one company for both can be the better choice

Using separate companies for towing and repair means two phone calls, two invoices, and possibly an extra storage fee while your car waits. A single provider keeps things simple. At Prairie Line, your tow can be credited toward qualifying repairs, and the technicians get notes from the driver about how the car failed. Questions to ask any provider

  • What is the total price, and what could change it?
  • Do you tow to the shop of my choice?
  • Is there a fee for storing my car overnight?
  • How long is the warranty on repairs?
  • Will I get a text when the driver is on the way?
