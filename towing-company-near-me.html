What happens when you call

  1. A dispatcher answers and asks where you are, what vehicle you drive, and what went wrong.
  2. You get a firm price quote and an arrival estimate before we send a truck.
  3. You receive a text with your driver's name and a link to watch the truck's progress.
  4. The driver checks your vehicle, secures it, and takes it where you choose: your home, another shop, or our repair shop.

Safety tips while you wait

Turn on your hazard lights and move as far from traffic as you safely can. On a busy road such as Interstate 80, stay in the car with your seat belt on unless there is a fire or another danger. Tell the dispatcher your nearest mile marker or exit number so we can find you fast.
