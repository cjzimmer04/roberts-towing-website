What services does Prairie Line Towing & Auto Repair provide?

Prairie Line provides towing, roadside assistance, accident recovery, and full-service auto repair for drivers in Iowa City, Coralville, North Liberty, and surrounding areas of Johnson County. Towing and roadside help are available 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Our repair shop is open six days a week.

Call (319) 555-0142

Towing

  • Flatbed towing for all-wheel-drive, electric, and low-clearance vehicles
  • Wheel-lift towing for standard cars, SUVs, pickups, and vans
  • Long-distance towing to a shop, dealership, or home of your choice
  • Private property towing for apartment complexes and business lots, with proper authorization

Roadside assistance

  • Jump starts for dead batteries
  • Spare tire changes
  • Fuel delivery when you run empty
  • Lockout help when your keys are inside the car

Most roadside calls are finished in under 30 minutes after our driver arrives.

Accident and recovery

  • Winching vehicles out of ditches, mud, and snowbanks
  • Accident scene towing, coordinated with police and your insurance company
  • Secure storage in our fenced, lit lot while claims are sorted out

Auto repair

  • Brakes, rotors, and brake fluid
  • Batteries, alternators, and starters
  • Engine diagnostics and check-engine lights
  • Cooling system repair, including radiators and water pumps
  • Tires, wheel alignment, and suspension work
  • Oil changes and scheduled maintenance
  • Minor collision and body repair

Our repair shop is led by Marisol Reyes, an ASE Master Technician. Every repair carries a 12-month, 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor.

How the services work together

You can use any of these services on its own, or combine them. If we tow your car to our shop and the repair costs $150 or more, the tow fee is credited toward your bill. Prices are quoted before any truck is sent, so there are no surprises. Dispatcher Tom Brandt and owner Dale Okafor are happy to answer questions by phone at any hour.
