Does Prairie Line do auto repair and towing? Yes, both

Many people are surprised to learn that a towing company can also fix the car. At Prairie Line Towing & Auto Repair, the same company that picks up your vehicle can repair it. Our tow trucks and our repair shop share one address at 214 Mill Race Road in Iowa City.

Call (319) 555-0142

Why having both under one roof helps

  • One phone call. You do not need to find a tow and then search for a shop while stranded.
  • No handoffs. Your car goes straight from the truck into our secured lot, and the shop has your notes from the driver.
  • Tow credit. When a repair costs $150 or more, we subtract the tow fee from your bill.
  • Safe storage. Vehicles that arrive after hours stay in our fenced, lit lot until the shop opens.

Repairs our shop handles

  • Brakes, rotors, and brake fluid
  • Batteries, alternators, and starters
  • Engine diagnostics and check-engine lights
  • Cooling system repair, including radiators and water pumps
  • Tires, wheel alignment, and suspension work
  • Oil changes and scheduled maintenance
  • Collision damage assessment and minor body repair

Our shop is led by Marisol Reyes, an ASE Master Technician with 15 years of experience. All repairs include a 12-month, 12,000-mile warranty on parts and labor.

Shop hours

The repair shop is open Monday through Friday from 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., and Saturday from 8 a.m. to noon. Towing is available 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including holidays.

Can I take my car somewhere else?

Absolutely. The tow is yours to direct. If you already have a trusted mechanic or a dealership in mind, tell the dispatcher and we will deliver your vehicle there. You only pay for the towing.
